About Nad Al Sheba Gardens

A gated Meraas community close to Meydan and Downtown.

Nad Al Sheba Gardens is a gated villa community by Meraas, built in phases around landscaped parks and a swimmable lagoon. Homes range from 3-bedroom townhouses to 4 to 7-bedroom villas with open-plan layouts, and the newer phases add larger villas facing the water and green spaces. Phases, layouts and availability differ, so tell Mark which phase or size you want and he will send what is available now.
