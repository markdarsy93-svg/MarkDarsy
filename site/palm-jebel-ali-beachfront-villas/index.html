Palm Jebel Ali FAQ

Questions buyers and owners ask.

Where can I buy a beachfront villa in Dubai?

Palm Jebel Ali is Dubai's newest beachfront villa address, with villas on twelve fronds built by Nakheel. As of 6 October 2026, 21 resale villas are advertised there from AED 18M to AED 53.9M. Mark Darsy, a Palm Jebel Ali resale specialist, shortlists up to three villas for each buyer.

How much is a beachfront mansion on Palm Jebel Ali?

Seven-bedroom Coral Collection villas and beach mansions on Palm Jebel Ali are advertised from AED 32.5M to AED 53.9M as of 6 October 2026. Coral Collection designs measure about 11,222–12,165 sq ft of built-up area in Nakheel's brochures. These are asking prices, not valuations or achieved sales.

What is the cheapest beachfront villa on Palm Jebel Ali?

The lowest advertised resale asking price on this site is AED 18M, for a five- or six-bedroom Beach Collection villa of about 7,300–7,900 sq ft. The cash needed at transfer also depends on how much the seller has already paid Nakheel.

Are Palm Jebel Ali villas luxury properties?

Yes. Every villa is a detached five- to seven-bedroom home on a frond, designed by studios including SAOTA, NAGA Architects, LW Design Group and LOCI Architecture, with built-up areas from about 7,316 to 12,165 sq ft in Nakheel's brochures.

Does a Palm Jebel Ali villa qualify for the UAE Golden Visa?

The UAE 10-year Golden Visa for property investors applies to property valued at AED 2 million or more, subject to approval by the relevant authorities. Every villa on this site is above that threshold. Nakheel sales centres offer Golden Visa facilitation, but off-plan and resale eligibility conditions are set by the authorities and should be confirmed for the specific villa.
