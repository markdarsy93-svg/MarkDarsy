How to compare

At this scale, the plot matters as much as the house.

01

Confirm the individual specification

Coral design names describe a collection, not an interchangeable product. Identify the release and exact villa, then check bedrooms, built-up area, plot dimensions, façade and floor plan against the documents.

  • Official floor plan and villa reference
  • Documented built-up and plot areas
  • Layout and bedroom relationships
02

Look beyond total square footage

Compare the width of the plot facing the shoreline, garden and pool arrangement, usable outdoor space and how principal rooms open towards the water.

03

Assess privacy and the view corridor

Review side setbacks, adjacent villa layouts, facing windows and terraces. Study the outlook against the current masterplan and site evidence rather than assuming a render guarantees a view.

  • Separation from neighbouring villas
  • Shoreline frontage and beach relationship
  • Visible and planned elements within the outlook
04

Example: similar scale, different usability

Illustrative comparison — not a live listing or completed transaction

Two Coral villas have similar built-up areas but different plot proportions and neighbouring terraces. One may suit large-scale entertaining; the other may need a closer privacy review.

05

Price the complete acquisition commitment

Review the agreed seller price, amount already paid, outstanding balance and dated instalment schedule together with transaction costs. Verify seller authority and transfer requirements before committing.

06

Source opportunities with a clear brief

Availability is confirmed villa by villa, including opportunities shared privately with owner permission when available. An introduction to a collection is not a promise of current inventory.

Palm Jebel Ali FAQ

Questions buyers and owners ask.

How big are Palm Jebel Ali Coral Collection villas?

Nakheel brochures list Coral Collection designs at about 11,222–12,165 sq ft of built-up area, mostly with seven bedrooms; Coral Dune and Sunset Mirage have six. Designs include Coral Living, Cranberry Sky, Porcelain Roses, Red Aurora, Coral Dune, Sunset Mirage, Hibiscus, Terracotta, Ruby Sunset and Redwood.

What is the difference between Beach Collection and Coral Collection villas?

Beach Collection villas are five- and six-bedroom homes with Nakheel brochure built-up areas of about 7,316–8,434 sq ft. Coral Collection villas are larger six- and seven-bedroom residences of about 11,222–12,165 sq ft. Designs come from studios including SAOTA, NAGA Architects, LW Design Group and LOCI Architecture. Compare them in the Beach and Coral design libraries.

Answers reflect the information on this website as of 6 October 2026. Prices, availability and official requirements change, so confirm them for the specific villa.
