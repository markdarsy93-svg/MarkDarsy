Palm Jebel Ali FAQ

Questions buyers and owners ask.

Is Palm Jebel Ali bigger than Palm Jumeirah?

Yes. Palm Jebel Ali is the larger of Nakheel's two palm islands. Its villas sit on twelve fronds, A–F and K–P, and Nakheel's plan lists 7 islands, about 120 km of coastline and more than 90 km of beachfront.

Who developed Palm Jumeirah and Palm Jebel Ali?

Both are Nakheel projects. Nakheel, part of Dubai Holding, built Palm Jumeirah and is now developing Palm Jebel Ali.

Is Palm Jebel Ali or Palm Jumeirah a better villa purchase?

They are different markets. Palm Jumeirah is an established, completed island priced on completed sales, condition and running costs. Palm Jebel Ali is under development, so pricing depends on the exact design, frond and the seller's payment position, and costs are driven by instalments and the outstanding balance.

Why buy Palm Jebel Ali instead of Palm Jumeirah?

Buyers choose Palm Jebel Ali for new, architect-designed detached villas of about 7,316–12,165 sq ft, and for an off-plan payment structure where part of the price is paid to Nakheel over time. Buyers who want a completed home to move into now usually prefer Palm Jumeirah. The right choice depends on timing, budget and how you will use the home.

How far is Palm Jebel Ali from Palm Jumeirah?

Nakheel lists the drive from Palm Jebel Ali to Palm Jumeirah at about 28 minutes, via Sheikh Zayed Road (E11).
