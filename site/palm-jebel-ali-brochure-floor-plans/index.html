Palm Jebel Ali FAQ

Questions buyers and owners ask.

Where can I get the Palm Jebel Ali brochure and floor plans?

Nakheel publishes a brochure, floor plans, a master plan, a gallery and a price and payment plan for the Beach and Coral Collections. Mark Darsy can send the current pack on WhatsApp (+971 58 541 4999), and this site lists all 27 villa designs with their brochure areas on the Beach Collection and Coral Collection pages.

How many villa designs are there on Palm Jebel Ali?

This site tracks 16 Beach Collection designs of about 7,316–8,434 sq ft and 11 Coral Collection designs of about 11,222–12,165 sq ft, taken from Nakheel's brochures. New releases add designs, so a few areas are still marked as to be confirmed.

What is the payment plan on a Palm Jebel Ali resale villa?

There is no single resale payment plan. The seller has already paid part of the developer price; you pay the agreed seller price at transfer and take over the remaining developer instalments on that villa's schedule. Ask for the villa's statement of account, which shows the amount paid, the balance and the date of each instalment. The purchase cost estimator helps compare two villas.

Do brochure floor plans match the villa I am buying?

Treat them as indicative. Areas can change between releases: Blue Horizon is 7,316.01 sq ft in its launch brochure and 7,307.73 sq ft in the latest release. The villa's SPA and its own floor plan and plot plan govern, so check them before an offer.

Are the brochure images of the actual villa?

No. Brochure and gallery images are developer conceptual renders of the collections. They do not show a specific resale villa, its plot or its current construction stage.

Answers reflect the information on this website as of 5 November 2026. Prices, availability and official requirements change, so confirm them for the specific villa.
