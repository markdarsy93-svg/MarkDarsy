How to read a frond

The frond is the start, not the answer.

Two sides per frond

Every frond has a sunrise side and a sunset side. Orientation belongs to the individual plot, never to the frond letter.

Spine to tip

Plots closer to the spine favour daily access; plots towards the tip trade a longer drive for a quieter, more open position.

Stage and timing

Fronds K–P are further along in construction. On a resale, timing changes how many developer instalments remain before handover.
