Is Frond K sunrise or sunset facing?

Both. Like every Palm Jebel Ali frond, Frond K has a sunrise side and a sunset side, and plots run from the spine towards the tip. The orientation belongs to the individual plot, so check the compass direction of the main rooms, garden and pool on the plot plan.
