How to compare

From design name to a sound decision.

01

Start with the exact design and release

A design name is not a complete specification. Identify the villa number and release, then match the floor plan, documented built-up area, plot area and façade.

  • Bedrooms, circulation and family space
  • Pool, garden and shoreline relationship
  • Documented area rather than rounded marketing figures
02

Compare two villas on the same framework

Separate what is documented, what the seller reports and what still needs checking. A lower headline price is then assessed, not assumed to be better value.

  • Frond, orientation and position along the frond
  • Plot relationship, outlook and neighbouring terraces
  • Seller price, paid amount, outstanding balance and instalment dates
03

Example: same design, different position

Illustrative comparison — not a live listing or completed transaction

Two villas share a design, but one sits closer to the spine and the other closer to the tip. A buyer prioritising daily access may prefer the first; one seeking a particular outlook may investigate the second. Neither position establishes a premium without plot evidence and comparable pricing.

04

Example: price and cash timing are different questions

Illustrative comparison — not a live listing or completed transaction

Two seller prices look similar, yet the villas have different amounts paid and different next instalment dates. Compare the amount payable at transfer, outstanding developer obligations and transaction costs separately.

Palm Jebel Ali FAQ

Questions buyers and owners ask.

How big are Palm Jebel Ali Beach Collection villas?

Nakheel brochures list Beach Collection designs at about 7,316–8,434 sq ft of built-up area, with five or six bedrooms (some five-bedroom designs add a family room). Designs include Blue Horizon, Sapphire, Tropical Mist, Acquamarina, Indigo Ocean, Mediterranean, Provence, Azure Blue, Pacific Breeze, Cyan Sky, Bluejay and Ocean Whisper.

What is the difference between Beach Collection and Coral Collection villas?

Beach Collection villas are five- and six-bedroom homes with Nakheel brochure built-up areas of about 7,316–8,434 sq ft. Coral Collection villas are larger six- and seven-bedroom residences of about 11,222–12,165 sq ft. Designs come from studios including SAOTA, NAGA Architects, LW Design Group and LOCI Architecture. Compare them in the Beach and Coral design libraries.

Answers reflect the information on this website as of 6 October 2026. Prices, availability and official requirements change, so confirm them for the specific villa.
